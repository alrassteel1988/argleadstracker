# Design Direction

## Purpose

Management-ready walkthrough of the Salesman mobile workflow for opening and using the AI Sales Assistant in ARG Leads Tracker.

## Visual System

- 1920x1080 landscape presentation with a centered iPhone-sized product capture.
- Flat Bauhaus CRM palette: deep navy, Al Ras gold, white, muted blue, and restrained green/red status accents.
- No gradients, blur, frosted glass, or decorative visual noise.
- Strong geometric frames, 2px borders, 8px maximum radius, and crisp shadows.
- Large executive captions on the left; real CRM mobile UI on the right.

## Motion

- Camera-like screenshot pushes use scale and position only.
- Section changes use short opacity and horizontal translation.
- Callouts use a hard gold ring and compact numbered labels.
- Motion remains readable and purposeful, with no bounce or elastic easing.

## Typography

- Inter or system sans-serif.
- Titles: 68-82px, 700 weight.
- Supporting copy: 28-34px, 500 weight.
- Step labels: 20-24px, uppercase, 700 weight.

## Accessibility

- High contrast text.
- All important meaning is repeated in captions, not color alone.
- Safe title margins exceed 64px.
