Meet the AI Sales Assistant inside the ARG Leads Tracker Salesman workspace.

From the mobile dashboard, the salesman keeps the day's leads, overdue calls, and next actions in view. Tap the AI Assistant launcher in the lower-right corner to open a voice-enabled command panel.

The assistant accepts spoken or typed requests. A salesman can ask to show overdue calls, check work due today, schedule a follow-up, or prepare an email draft.

Read-only questions return results immediately and only from records the salesman is permitted to see.

For CRM changes, the assistant gathers missing details and presents the action for review before anything is saved.

That means faster follow-up, cleaner records, and controlled automation from any phone.
